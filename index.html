What we do

OUR MISSION

Town Center laundromat opened in January of 2007. We take pride in having one of the cleanest and best kept laundry facilities in the Portland area.

OPEN 24 HOURS A DAYS / 7 DAYS A WEEK

Our laundromat offers 24 HOURS A DAYS / 7 DAYS A WEEK services with FREE Wi-Fi access, 24hr off-site security monitoring, beverage and snack vending machines and lots of extra large washers and dryers.

OUr location

We are located at 8301 SE Monterey Ave. Happy Valley OR 97086. Close to the corner of SE 82nd and SE Monterey Ave. in Clackamas Oregon. Right next to the Clackamas Town Center. Ample free parking is available.
